# RPG With No Name
A Placeholder Name &mdash; Could be OSR merged with current popular RPGs.

## Goals

- Works like D&D 5e and Pathfinder 2e but simpler.
- GM decides things, minimize rules lawyering.
- Focus on the Pillars of gaming. 
- The four pillar scores replace the traditional six ability scores and the skills.
- Use the Support pillar outside downtime, including healing and most spell-casting.
- Starting 1st-level characters pick a pillar to focus on: this is their “class”.
- 1st-level characters can pick a “focus” (e.g., Archer focus for Combatant class).
- Specialization beyond the starting focus can be negotiated with the GM or taken from defaults
- Advancement is quicker and more capped than D&amp;D or Pathfinder
- Map classes to roles:
  - Combatant (controller/tank/striker)
  - Explorer (ranger/rogue/scout)
  - Speaker (face)
  - Scholar (healer/utility/wizard)
- When in doubt and equivalent, prefer Advantage over flat bonuses &mdash; more table enjoyment
- **Favorite Pathfinder feature:** 3 action economy
- **Favorite D&D feature:** Advantage/Disadvantage

## All Characters

You may use any non-magical item, including weapons and armor. You may freely use any simple or common item. Any other item is used with _Disadvantage_.

### Pick Who You Are

Bring your ancestry, species, or background and negotiate with the GM if it fits the campaign. Use resources from D&amp;D or Pathfinder or other RPG, or your imagination. You are what you are.

All characters get 3 free minor features of your choice from who you are subject to the GM accepting them. Humans get 4.

If you are not sure what to suggest, here are examples based on other published RPG games:

- An extra language for the campaign.
- Leshy (Pathfinder): _Superstition_ &mdash; You gain a +1 to saves against effects that would control or charm you, and you can automatically identify edible plants in natural environments.
- Mouse (Mouse Guard): _Home Guard_ &mdash; Once per session, when you fail a check related to protecting your home or companions, you may reroll the check. You must keep the second result.
- Rock Gnome (D&amp;D): _Handy_ &mdash; You have proficiency with tools and can spend 1 hour and 10 credits/gp of materials to construct a Tiny clockwork device (AC 5, 1 hp) that performs one simple action (noise, light, or movement) for up to 24 hours.

### Starting Pillar Scores

You can choose 2 ways to setup your initial pillar scores. After you may add +1 to any score as a free boost.

#### Choose Some

Assign these for your initial pillar scores: 14, 14, 10, 10.

#### Buy Scores

You start with an initial budget of 18 points. Spend on scores until you run out of points.

| Score | Cost |
| :---: | :--: |
| 8 | 0 |
| 9 | 1 |
| 10 | 2 |
| 11 | 3 |
| 12 | 4 |
| 13 | 5 |
| 14 | 7 |

#### Species and Background

Coming from D&amp;D or Pathfinder, map your ancestry/species and background ability scores increases:

| D&amp;D | Pillar |
| :------ | :----- |
| Strength | Combat or Support |
| Dexterity | Combat or Exploration |
| Constitution | Combat or Support |
| Intelligence | Exploration or Support |
| Wisdom | Exploration or Social or Support |
| Charisma | Social |

Using Strength for Support is interesting. You don&rsquo;t just hit things: you can push and lift.

#### Best You Can Do

Regardless of your pillar scores, you can never have a bonus above +5 from these. You may have other features or magic items to go higher. Best of luck to you.

## Classes

Classes are the primary pillar a character focuses on. At 1<sup>st</sup>-level you choose one class (pillar) and one starting Focus within it. You may add another class and Focus at any level gain.

\page

## Combatant

You master the Combat pillar. You freely use any armor or weapon. You roll initiative with _Advantage_.

## Focuses

### Archer

You excel at ranged combat. You use ranged weapons with _Advantage_ when a target is within normal range.

### Guardian

You excel at protection and holding the line. When an close-by ally is hit by an attack, you may use your reaction to become the target instead.

### Skirmisher

You excel at mobile melee and opportunistic strikes. When you hit with a melee weapon, you may move up to 10 feet with no opportunity attacks from your opponents.

### Duelist

You excel at single-target precision and defense. When you attack a creature and no other creature is next to it, roll with Advantage. You may do this once per round.

\page

## Explorer

You master the Exploration pillar. You freely use any armor that is not heavy, and any weapon. You roll initiative with _Advantage_.

## Focuses

### Scout

Stealth, reconnaissance, and avoiding notice. You have Advantage on Stealth checks. When you are hidden and make an attack, you do not automatically reveal your position if the attack misses. Once per encounter you may take the Hide action as a bonus action.

### Pathfinder

Navigation, tracking, and wilderness travel. You and allies within 30 feet travel at a faster pace without the usual penalties (no disadvantage on Perception, no increased chance of exhaustion). Once per day you may automatically find a safe route, shortcut, or source of fresh water or shelter.

### Delver

Traps, locks, and dungeon hazards. You have Advantage on checks to find or disable traps and to open locks.

### Surveyor

Perception, mapping, and environmental detail. You have Advantage sensing around and accurately map areas with those senses as you pass through.

\page

## Speaker

You master the Social pillar.

## Focuses

### Negotiator

Persuasion, deals, and calming. You have Advantage on checks to persuade or reach a peaceful agreement.

### Infiltrator

Deception, disguise, and false identities. You have Advantage on Deception checks to maintain a disguise or false identity.

### Orator

Inspiration, leadership, and public presence. Once per encounter, when you address a group or a key listener, you may shift the their attitude one step friendlier, or grant your allies Advantage on their next Social check involving that group.

### Confidant

Insight, counsel, and building trust. You have Advantage on Insight checks and on checks to calm or reassure a willing creature.

\page

## Scholar

You master the Support pillar (healing, crafting, research, recovery, projects).

## Focuses

### Healer

Recovery, treatment, and stabilization. Add your Support modifier to healing you perform.

### Crafter

Creating, repairing, and improving items. Crafting take half either the usual time or cost (your choice).

### Scholar

Research, lore, and ritual preparation. You have Advantage on research checks and may prepare one extra minor magical effect during downtime.

### Steward

Resources, holdings, and long-term management. A place you manage produces one extra useful resource (supplies, coin, or information) each downtime period.

## Advancement

At each character level increase you get 3 character points:

- Increase any pillar score by +1 (2 points)
- Gain a new feature suitable to your level to negotiatiate with your GM (1 point)
- Acquire a new magic item suitable to your level (1 point)
