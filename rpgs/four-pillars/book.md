# RPG With No Name
A Placeholder Name &mdash; Could be OSR merged with current popular RPGs.

## Goals

- The GM runs the show including rulings on rules and character features.
- Focus on the Pillars of gaming. Add a Support pillar. Replace the traditional six ability scores and skills with pillar scores.
- Deemphasize Skills. Time-consuming at table, and hard for players to manage.
- Works like D&D 5.5e and Pathfinder 2e but simpler.
- Add-in design. So game rules like Exhaustion from D&D or modular feats from Pathfinder work.
- Build out the Support pillar outside of downtime. Include healing and much spell-casting.
- Characters can focus and specialize from their start.
- Level 1-10 rather than 1-20.
- **Favorite D&D feature:** Advantage/Disadvantage over flat bonuses
- **Favorite Pathfinder feature:** 3 Action economy over Bonus Actions

### Design Notes

- Map classes to roles:
  - Combatant (controller/tank/striker)
  - Explorer (ranger/rogue/scout)
  - Speaker (face) &mdash; more application? What about magic?
  - Scholar (healer/utility/casters)

#### Guardian

- The current Guardian mixes single-ally protection (Attack Me, Best Buddy) with a light Social taunt. This overlaps with the Duelist’s single-target aggro tools.Reconsider later:
- Keep Guardian as the single-ally protector and move any area-effect taunt or “protect the group” features to a new Combatant Focus (e.g. Vanguard or Sentinel), or
Shift Guardian toward local AoE protection (allies within 5–10 feet) and leave pure single-target “fight me” to the Duelist.
- Decide before locking higher-level Guardian features.

## The Four Pillars

- Combat
  - Direct conflict
  - Weapon use
  - Tactical positioning
  - Overcoming opponents through force or precision
  - Crowd Control &mdash; should this be for Support pillar?
- Exploration
  - Navigation
  - Perception
  - Stealth
  - Traps
  - Environmental challenges
  - Mapping
  - Discovering what the world contains
- Social
  - Persuasion
  - Deception
  - Negotiation
  - Insight
  - Reputation
  - Leadership
  - Resolving situations through people
- Support
  <br/>Lots of rules in RPGs around this pillar, but not explicity called out by them as such.
  - Healing
  - Recovery
  - Crafting
  - Research
  - Projects
  - Home base
  - Minions
  - Crowd Control &mdash; TODO: is this only for Combat?

## For the Player

### All Characters

You may use any non-magical item, including weapons and armor. You may use any simple weapon or common item. Other items are usable with _Disadvantage_. Each class has more specific details.

### Pick Who You Are

Choose a background and an ancestry, race, or species. Negotiate with the GM so it fits the campaign.

All characters receive 3 minor features tied to who they are. These can be drawn from background; ancestry, race, or species; or backstory.

Describe what you look like, what you did before adventuring, and who or what matters to you.

### Starting Pillar Scores

You can choose 2 ways to setup your initial pillar scores. After you may add +1 to any score as a free boost.

#### Choose Some

Assign these for your initial pillar scores: 14, 14, 10, 10.

#### Buy Scores

You start with an initial budget of 18 points. Spend on scores until you run out of points.

| Score | Cost |
| :---: | :--: |
| 8 | 0 |
| 9 | 1 |
| 10 | 2 |
| 11 | 3 |
| 12 | 4 |
| 13 | 5 |
| 14 | 7 |
| 15 | 9 |
| 16 | 12 |

#### Species and Background

Coming from D&amp;D or Pathfinder, map your ancestry/species and background ability score increases:

| D&amp;D | Pillar |
| :------ | :----- |
| Strength | Combat or Support |
| Dexterity | Combat or Exploration |
| Constitution | Combat or Support |
| Intelligence | Exploration or Support |
| Wisdom | Exploration or Social or Support |
| Charisma | Social |

Using Strength for Support is interesting. You don&rsquo;t just hit things: you can push and lift.

#### Best You Can Do

Regardless of your pillar scores, you can never have a bonus above +5 from these. You may have other features or magic items to go higher. Best of luck to you.

### On Your Turn

You get 3 Actions. Most choices cost 1 Action: move, attack, cast a simple spell, use an item, hide, so on. You also get 1 Reaction, usable an another creature&rsquo;s turn.

Some choices are free such as drawing or dropping a single weapon or item. Some choices cost 2 or 3 Actions. A few choices may require Actions across multiple turns.

Your GM decides on what makes sense at the table.

#### Attacking Again

When you use your Actions to attack more than once:

- Any character can attack a second time as one of their 3 Actions, but with _Disadvantage_.
- Some characters can attack the second time without _Disadvantage_, or make a third attack.

## Combatant

You master the Combat pillar.

#### 1 Level

**Heavy Training.** You freely use light, medium, or heavy armor and all weapons, and equivalent magic items. Other items are at _Disadvantage_ to use without further training.

**On the Offense.** You are better at multiple attacks and gain more of them. Still some weapons may take more than one attack Action to use against opponents.

| Levels | Benefits |
|:------:|:---------|
| 3 | 2<sup>nd</sup> attack Action is not at _Disadvantage_ |
| 5 | Gain a 3<sup>rd</sup> Action attack at _Disadvantage_ |
| 6 | All weapons only need 1 Attack action to strike with |
| 7 | 3<sup>rd</sup> attack Action is not at _Disadvantage_ |
| 9 | A 4<sup>th</sup> attack Action at _Disadvantage_ as a Free Action |

**Quick To Act.** You roll initiative with _Advantage_.

**Pillar Focus.** Choose a Combatant Focus.

### Generalist

You trained in all styles of Combat.

#### 1 Level

**Apprentice Attacker.** Once per encounter you have _Advantage_ on any attack.

**Improvised Weapon.** No suitable item in reach is unusable as a melee weapon. Improvised melee weapons deal d4 damage.

#### 2 Levels

**Journeyman Attacker.** Once per encounter you have _Advantage_ on all attacks during your turn.

### Archer

You excel at ranged combat.

#### 1 Level

**Better Aim.** You use ranged weapons with _Advantage_ when a target is within normal range.

**Ranged Improvised Weapon.** No suitable item in reach is unusable as a ranged weapon. Improvised ranged weapons deal d6 damage with a 20 foot range.

#### 2 Levels

**Faster Shots.** Your second ranged attack each turn does not have _Disadvantage_ if the target is within normal range.

### Duelist

You excel at single-target precision.

#### 1 Level

**Single Target.** When you attack a creature and no other creature is next to it, roll with _Advantage_. You may do this once per turn.

**Come to Me.** Once per encounter you can call out a target up to 60 feet away to approach you best it can to within 5 feet of you.

**Call Out.** Once per encounter, when you hit a creature or use Come to Me, you may taunt it. Make a Social check; on a success the target has _Disadvantage_ on attacks against anyone other than you until the end of its next turn.

#### 2 Levels

**Stronger Strikes.** When you hit a creature and no other creature is next to it, add your Combat modifier to the damage a second time.

### Guardian

You excel at protection and holding the line. 

#### 1 Level

**Attack Me.** When an ally next to you is hit by an attack, you may use your Reaction to become the target instead and take the damage or effects.

**Hold the Line.** Once per encounter, when you use Attack Me or Missed by an Inch, you can taunt the attacker. Make a Social check; on a success the attacker has _Disadvantage_ on its next attack.

**Missed by an Inch.**. Once per encounter you can increase your AC by 5 as a Reaction.

#### 2 Levels

**Best Buddy.** When an ally within 5 feet of you is hit by an attack, you can use your Reaction to impose _Disadvantage_ on the attack roll possibly turning the hit into a miss.

**Push Back.** When you use your Reaction to take a hit for an ally, you may also push the attacker 5 feet or knock it prone (your choice).

\page

### Hand to Hand

Your fists are your weapons.

#### 1 Level

**Dangerous Hands.** Your hands attack as d8 weapons.

**Elemental Hands.** Once per encounter your fists take elemental aspects and deal that damage type for all attacks.

**Improved Improvised Weapon.** No suitable item in reach is unusable as a melee weapon. Improvised melee weapons deal d6 damage.

#### 2 Levels

**Hazardous Hands.** Your hits are harder. Each turn double the damage dice on the first successful attack using your fists.

### Skirmisher

You excel at mobile melee and opportunistic strikes. 

#### 1 Level

**Nimble Feet.** When you are damaged from melee, you can react by moving up to 10 feet, and those in melee cannot attack you.

**Speedy Feet.** Once per encounter you can dash with double your movement speed.

#### 2 Levels

**Dangerous Quarry.** When you are damaged from melee and react by moving, you may freely attack one time after moving.

## Explorer

You master the Exploration pillar.

#### 1 Level

**Medium Training.** You freely use any light or medium armor, and any weapon that is not two-handed, and equivalent magic items. Other items are at _Disadvantage_ to use without further training.

**Quick to Act.** You roll initiative with _Advantage_.

**Pillar Focus.** Choose an Explorer Focus.

## Focuses

### Generalist

You trained in all styles of Exploration.

#### 1 Level

**Apprentice Explorer.** Once per day you have _Advantage_ on any exploration check.

**Improvised Tool.** No suitable item in reach is unusable as a tool for exploration. Improvised tools work without _Disadvantage_.

#### 2 Levels

**Journeyman Explorer.** Once per day you have _Advantage_ on all exploration checks during a single scenario or stretch of travel (up to 4 hours).

### Delver

Traps, locks, and dungeon hazards.

#### 1 Level

**Clever Fingers.** You have _Advantage_ on checks to find or disable traps and to open locks.

**Starting Equipment.** You have a pickaxe. You can use this to delve or as a weapon.

#### 2 Levels

**Well Spotted.** Once per day you may find or disable a trap, or open a lock, as a Free Action.

**Hazardous Terrain.** Once per day you can spend 10 minutes working a 20' by 20' area of ground to turn it into difficult terrain.

### Scout

Stealth, reconnaissance, and avoiding notice.

#### 1 Level

**Hard to Notice.** You have _Advantage_ on being stealthy.

**Attack From Behind.** When you are hidden and make an attack, you do not automatically reveal your position if the attack misses.

**Safer Spot.** Once per encounter you can hide as a Free Action.

##### 2 Levels

**Quick Cover.** You may hide as a Free Action once per turn (instead of once per encounter).

### Pathfinder

Navigation, tracking, and wilderness travel.

#### 1 Level

**Quick Guidance.** You and allies within 30 feet travel at a faster pace without the usual penalties (no _Disadvantage_ on perception, no increased chance of exhaustion).

**Safe Harbor.** Once per day you may automatically find a safe route, shortcut, or source of fresh water or shelter.

#### 2 Levels

**Sure Path.** While you lead the party, difficult terrain does not slow you or your allies, and the party has _Advantage_ on checks to avoid becoming lost or exhausted from travel.

### Surveyor

Perception, mapping, and environmental detail.

#### 1 Level

**Good Observer.** You automatically keep usable notes and a rough map of areas you travel through to  document terrain, paths, or major landmarks.

**Good Listener.** Within 1 day of a conversation, you can spend an Action to reconstruct full, accurate notes of what was said without losing key details.

**Starting Equipment.** You begin with paper or parchment, a quill, and ink if you do not already have these.

#### 2 Levels

**Keen Eye.**  Once per day within range of your senses you may notice one hidden detail, secret, or environmental hazard without a check.

**Expert Observer.** Once per day your maps or notes give _Advantage_ to their readers for Exploration checks of what you noted.

## Scholar

You master the Support pillar (healing, crafting, research, recovery, projects).

#### 1 Level

**Light Training.** You freely use any armor that is light and any simple weapons, and equivalent magic items. Other items are at _Disadvantage_ to use without further training.

**Doctoral Student.** You have _Advantage_ on research checks and may prepare one extra minor magical effect when you craft.

**Field Kit.** Once per day you can spend an Action to produce a minor consumable (healing salve, antitoxin, or temporary tool) that grants the consumer _Advantage_ on one relevant check or restores hit points equal to your Support modifier (minimum 1). It remains usable for 1 day.

**Pillar Focus.** Choose a Scholar Focus.

## Focuses

### Generalist

You trained in all means of Support.

#### 1 Level

**Well Read.** Once per day you can freely identify one item drawing on your broad scholarship. This takes 1 hour of interrupted study to fully identify the item, or 10 minutes to determine if the item is cursed and identify one non-obvious property. You can do this more often in a day as you gain levels as a Scholar:

| Levels | Uses per Day |
|:------:|:------------:|
| 1 | 1 |
| 4 | 2 |
| 8 | 3 |

#### 2 Levels

**Broad Knowledge.** Once per day you may treat a failed Support check as a success.

### Healer

Recovery, treatment, and stabilization.

#### 1 Level

**Field Care.** During a rest, you can treat yourself or another resting creature within reach a number of times per day equal to your Support modifier (minimum 1). Treatment of your target:
- Stabilizes at 0 hit points.
- Regains hit points equal to your Support modifier (minimum 1).
- Loses one level of exhaustion.

**Nurse.** Add your Support modifier to healing you perform.

#### 2 Levels

**Rapid Treatment.** Once per encounter you may heal a creature as a Free Action instead of an Action.

### Crafter

Creating, repairing, and improving items. 

#### 1 Level

**Apprentice Crafter.** Crafting takes half either the usual time or cost (your choice).

#### 2 Levels

**Journeyman Crafter.** Once per day you may complete a crafting project that would normally take up to 1 hour as a Free Action. In addition, when you choose to halve either the time or the cost of a project (from Apprentice Crafter), you may also reduce the other by 25%.

### Steward

Resources, holdings, and long-term management.

#### 1 Level

**Manager.** A place you manage produces one extra useful resource (supplies, coin, or information) each downtime period.

#### 2 Levels

**Reliable Holdings.** Once per downtime period you may double useful resources produced by a place you manage.

## Speaker

You master the Social pillar.

#### 1 Level

**Attention to Detail.** Once per day you can make a Social check in place of an Exploration check when you may have picked up information on this from others.

**Light Training.** You freely use any armor that is light and any simple weapons, and equivalent magic items. Other items are at _Disadvantage_ to use without further training.

**Pillar Focus.** Choose a Speaker Focus.

## Focuses

### Generalist

You trained in all styles of Social interaction.

#### 1 Level

**Apprentice Speaker.** Once per day you have _Advantage_ on one Social check.

**Medium Training.** You freely use any light or medium armor, and any weapon that is not two-handed, and equivalent magic items. Other items are at _Disadvantage_ to use without further training.

**Read the Room.** Once per encounter you may ask the GM one yes/no question for a truthful answer about a creature&rsquo;s current attitude or immediate goal.

#### 2 Levels

**Journeyman Speaker.** Once per day you have _Advantage_ on all Social checks during a single encouter.

### Confidant

Insight, counsel, and building trust.

#### 1 Level

**Thoughtful Insight.** You have _Advantage_ on insight checks and on checks to calm or reassure a willing creature.

#### 2 Levels

**Trusted Counsel.** Once per day, after a conversation, you learn one personal secret, fear, or desire of a creature you spoke with. The creature knows you know.

### Infiltrator

Deception, disguise, and false identities.

#### 1 Level

**Fake Face.** You have _Advantage_ on Deception checks to maintain a disguise or false identity.

#### 2 Levels

**Slip the Net.** Once per encounter, when your false identity or disguise is about to be exposed, you may reroll one failed deception check. You must use the new result.

### Negotiator

Persuasion, deals, and calming.

#### 1 Level

**Apprentice Negotiator.** You have _Advantage_ on checks to persuade or reach a peaceful agreement.

#### 2 Levels

**Favorable Terms.** Once per day, when you successfully reach a peaceful agreement, you may add one minor additional concession from the other party. The GM decides what is reasonable.

**Read the Room.** When you spend at least 1 minute observing a social gathering or negotiation, you learn one useful fact about the power dynamics, hidden motives, or recent events that the participants know. You have _Advantage_ on the next Social check you make in that scenario.

### Orator

Inspiration, leadership, and public presence.

#### 1 Level

**Persuasive Voice.** Once per encounter, when you address a group or a key listener, you may shift their disposition to you one step friendlier, or grant your allies _Advantage_ on their next Social check involving that group.

#### 2 Levels

**Leading Presence.** Once per encounter, when you address a group, you may grant all allies within earshot _Advantage_ on their next check or saving throw before the end of their next turns.

**Command the Room.** Once per day, when you address a group for at least 1 minute, you have _Advantage_ on the next Social check you make to inspire, persuade, or intimidate that group.

## Advancement

At each character level increase you get 3 character points:

- Increase any pillar score by +1 (2 points)
- Gain a new feature suitable to your level to discuss with your GM (1 point)
- Acquire a new magic item suitable to your level (1 point)

## For the Game Master
}}

### Monsters

Use the creature’s strongest attack bonus, armor, and hit points to set its Combat score. Map Perception, Stealth, and special senses to Exploration. Map any social or leadership traits to Social. Map healing, minions, or long-term effects to Support. Then rewrite its key abilities in the new format. The same steps work for D&D 5.5e, Pathfinder 2e, or most other fantasy RPGs.
