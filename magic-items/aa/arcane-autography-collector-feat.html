Prerequisite: Possession of or [rules]Attunement[/rules] to at least one Arcane Autography volume

Your obsession with the scattered works of the mysterious "Q" has borne sweet and bitter fruits. As you hold and attune to more volumes of this alphabetic series, their text and commentary grants you increasing arcane power. Under the "Features" tab of this feat on your character sheet, select the option that matches the number of volumes you currently have attuned.

These are not books to be lightly tossed aside. They should be thrown with great force.
— Q

Arcane Autography Volumes

Introduction If you must, start here. I prefer the finish.

A–D Aganazzar, [lore]Bigby[/lore], and others who blew themselves up or became footnotes. Good reading, if you dig. Use your hands. Hard to find this volume but not challenging.

[lore]Elminster[/lore] He gets his own volume. He insisted. I let him write half himself — the worse half. Then I crossed it out. Then he erased everything and I’m left with an empty reference book. Quite hard to find this volume. He let me create only so many copies.

E–J A loose–lipped gossiper, an obsessive for her pet, poor weather, and delightfully grasping tentacles — who’s bringing the cockatrices? If you expected [lore]Iggwilv[/lore] here, go see the [lore]Tasha[/lore] volume. You didn’t know her that well. Hard to find this volume but not challenging.

L–M Pleasant Leomund, and Melf’s obsession with acidic geometries. Draw them out in conversation or you lose their attention. Hard to find this volume but not challenging.

[lore]Mordenkainen[/lore] He turned out more notable wizard apprentices than anyone else. A natural born teacher, and we know what those are like. And the rudest person outside the planes. Quite hard to find this volume. They keep getting acquired.

N–O This is the group at dinner parties who get out of hand. [lore]Otto[/lore] knows how to get things swinging — and dancing. Avoid [lore]Otiluke[/lore] after he’s had a few. Hard to find this volume but not challenging.

[lore]Tasha[/lore] Dear Tasha, let’s do dinner sometime. I’ll bring the flowers if you bring the terror and passion.

R–Y This volume’s a mess. Blame Nystul. Or Rary. Or me. Just don’t ask about "P". And don’t trust Rary. Be frightened of [lore]Yolande[/lore] and she’ll appreciate you. Hard to find this volume but not challenging.

Z I hesitate to write this volume. You could be cut, burned, pierced, hung. Might as well live. I wrote this only once and need more drink.
