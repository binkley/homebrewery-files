This well-used tome smells faintly of ozone and lilac ink and is a cornucopia of magic history. Each page reveals arcane history that changed the world, and some pages are in the original caster’s own hand. Only spell authors A–D are present, and you are clearly missing other volumes. The author of this compendium series is unknown — styling themself as "Q".

The book is one of a set of volumes. The Introduction is missing — unless, of course, you found it. You suspect that without more of the set, you may not benefit fully though this volume may benefit enough. You may need the Introduction to aid in finding other volumes.

Copying a new spell into blank pages takes the same time and gold cost as writing it into a normal spellbook.

  • You gain a +1 bonus to [rules]spell attack[/rules] rolls and to your spell save [rules]difficulty class[/rules].

Living Archive

Once per long rest when attuned, you can ask the book a question about a named spell within its pages. A cryptic marginal note appears — you are unsure who or what wrote the note. It can give clues similar to the [spells]Augury[/spells] spell as the DM decides. If interpreting the note requires a [rules]D20 test[/rules], roll with [rules]advantage[/rules].

Of course it writes back. All the worst relationships do. So why do you talk to it?
— Q.

Unexpected Apprenticeship

While you are attuned to this book, when you gain a level in a spellcasting class, a new spell appears in its pages whether fresh ink on a blank page or a foreign leaf sewn in with haste.

At the DM’s discretion, the spell is one associated with a caster named in this volume, or another fitting entry. It may be of a higher level than you can currently cast.

I saw a new page write itself.

This is a séance. Even as I write, unexpected spells turn up as last regrets and apologies. At least it means they share ink too smug to erase.
— Q.

Spells Already Present

When you come across this book and cast [spells]Detect Magic[/spells], you discover that it is a magical spellbook containing scribed spells and commentary. The pages are organized by author, and follow with blank pages suitable for copying in new spells from any school or other authors.

Casting [spells]Identify[/spells] reveals details for the spells already written in the book including originals in an author’s hand. You can prepare or cast a spell from the book only if it appears in your class’s spell list.
