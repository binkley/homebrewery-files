Features

Knowledge is Power

The knowledge and commentary in this book enhance your spellcasting when you have [rules]attunement[/rules]. You sense power from original signatures and first-hand writings.

  • You gain a +1 bonus to [rules]spell attack[/rules] rolls and to your spell save [rules]difficulty class[/rules].
  • Once per week, you can cast [spells]Legend Lore[/spells] without expending a spell slot, but only for information about a person appearing in volumes of this series that you possess — and trust me, everyone worth writing about is famous.

Living Archive

Once per long rest when attuned, you can ask the book a question about a named spell within its pages. A cryptic marginal note appears — you are unsure who or what wrote the note. It can give clues similar to the [spells]Augury[/spells] spell as determined by the DM. If interpreting the note requires a [rules]D20 test[/rules], roll with [rules]advantage[/rules].

Of course it writes back. All the worst relationships do. So why do you talk to it?

Unexpected Apprenticeship

While you are attuned to this book, when you gain a level in a spellcasting class, a new spell appears in its pages whether fresh ink on a blank page or a foreign leaf sewn in with haste.

As determined by the DM the spell is one associated with a caster named in this volume, or another fitting spell. It may be of a higher level than you can currently cast.
