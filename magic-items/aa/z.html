Curse

This book is cursed, and becoming attuned to it extends the curse to you.

While you are cursed, the book occupies one of your attunement slots but grants absolutely no benefits. You become obsessively protective of it, unwilling to part with it, and loathe to change, alter, or mar it in any way — including writing on or in it (including spells), folding its leaves, or altering its binding. You treat any attempt by another creature to handle or alter the book as a direct threat and act to stop them.

A [spells]Remove Curse[/spells] spell ends the curse on you, which allows you to unattune from the book and part with it. Afterward, you may possess the book for benefits not requiring attunement.
