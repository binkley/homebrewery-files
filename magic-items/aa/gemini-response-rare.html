When you have one or more Arcane Autography volumes, add the Arcane Autography Collector feat to your character sheet to track your scaling benefits.

This thick volume serves both as a [items]spellbook[/items] and a [rules]Spellcasting Focus[/rules]. It inherits the baseline traits of the series: it cannot be damaged by fire or water, nor does it deteriorate with age. This rare item requires [rules]Attunement[/rules].

The book is one of a set of alphabetic volumes. The Introduction is missing—unless, of course, you found it. Without more of the set, you cannot benefit fully from its hidden cross-references, though its standalone power is formidable.

Copying a new spell into blank pages takes the same time and gold cost as writing it into a normal spellbook, and requires you to use [items]Calligrapher’s Supplies[/items] to match the script of the previous authors.

Features

Knowledge is Power

The commentary in this book enhances your spellcasting. You gain a +1 bonus to [rules]Spell Attack[/rules] rolls and to your spell save DC ([rules]Difficulty Class[/rules]).

Living Archive

Once per [rules]Long Rest[/rules], you can ask the book a question about a named spell within its pages. A cryptic marginal note appears in the hand of the spell’s creator. It provides clues similar to the [spells]Augury[/spells] spell. If interpreting the note requires a [rules]D20 Test[/rules], you roll with [rules]Advantage[/rules].

Of course it writes back. All the worst relationships do. So why do you talk to it?
— Q.

Unexpected Apprenticeship

While you are attuned to this book, when you gain a level in a spellcasting class, a new spell associated with a caster named in this volume appears somewhere in its pages. At the DM’s discretion, it may be of a higher level than you can currently cast.
