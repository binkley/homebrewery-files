# Tame Mimic

After time and effort taming a wild Mimic, you have a somewhat loyal pet. The size and abilities of your tame Mimic vary. 

A tame Mimic is smarter than a bird, cat or dog, but is still a wild aberration.

## Training

Training an Aberration such as a mimic requires convincing its predatory mind that cooperation yields safer food than eating its trainer. Once tamed, a mimic accepts only one master and demands daily care to remain loyal.

### Leash (Control)

Taming requires daily interaction over a set duration based on the mimic's size category. Choose one or more methods during training:

_**Coercion (Magic):**_ Spells like _Geas_ (30-day duration), _Modify Memory_, or _Charm Monster_ make feeding safe and grant automatic success on weekly taming checks.

_**Conditioning (Checks):**_ Spend at least 1 complete hour daily with the mimic. At the end of each week, make a DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) or Wisdom (Animal Handling) check (or use the _Influence_ action). Three total failures reset all progress and trigger an immediate attack. (This check is also needed when _Bloodied_.)

_**Bribery (Delicacies):**_ Offering delicacies grants _Advantage_ on weekly checks (10 gp of coins/gems or 50 gp of magic items). Each 200 gp of delicacies consumed reduces total taming time by 1 week.

### Collar (Attunement)

When attuning choose Strength, Wisdom, or Charisma.

The mimic uses your chosen modifier for its attack rolls and its **Mimic Save DC**: _8 + your Proficiency Bonus + chosen modifier_. For all other checks and traits, the mimic uses its own ability scores.

Once attuned, it binds to your voice and scent:

_**Trainer's Speech:**_ The mimic understands your spoken voice in the language you trained it in, obeying your commands. It does not understand other creatures.

_**New Friends:**_ During a Short or Long Rest, you can command the mimic to recognize up to a number of creatures equal to your chosen modifier as allies, obeying their commands and treating them as non-prey.

_**Softer Stomach:**_ On command, the mimic suppresses its digestive acids, allowing nonmagical objects to be safely stored in its cavity.

_**Selective Adhesion:**_ On command, the mimic suppresses its Adhesive trait for you and approved allies.

## Obedience

A tamed Mimic obeys verbal commands within 30 feet. Beyond 30 feet, it follows its last command to the best of its limited intelligence.

_**Self-Preservation:**_ Issuing a directly self-destructive or life-ending command ends attunement immediately, causing the mimic to revert to a wild state.

_**Mimic Mood:**_ When the mimic drops to half its maximum Hit Points or lower (_Bloodied_), make a _Conditioning_ check. On a failure, attunement breaks and the mimic turns hostile.

### Tame Mimic Features

#### Hatchling (Tiny / Uncommon)

* _**Pocket Ambush:**_ As a **Bonus Action**, you command your tame Mimic to bite a creature within 5 feet of you. Make a melee item attack using your Proficiency Bonus + your chosen ability modifier. On a hit, the target takes 1d4 Piercing damage + 1d4 Acid damage.
* _**Concealed Carrier:**_ You have **Advantage** on Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) checks to conceal the mimic on your person, and Wisdom (Perception) checks made by other creatures to notice or discern the mimic&rsquo;s true nature have **Disadvantage**.
* _**Digestive Disposal:**_ During a **Short Rest**, you can command your tame Mimic to consume and dissolve nonmagical objects weighing up to 1 lb (such as parchment, copper keys, or iron locks), destroying them without a trace.

#### Juvenile (Small / Rare)

* _**Pseudopod Strike:**_ As a **Bonus Action**, you command your tame Mimic to bite or strike a creature within 5 feet of you. Make a melee item attack using your Proficiency Bonus + your chosen ability modifier. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Piercing damage + 1d8 Acid damage.
* _**Anti-Theft Trap:**_ Unless commanded in detail otherwise, your tame Mimic uses a **Reaction** to snap shut on any creature attempting to touch, open or pick up the mimic. The triggering creature must succeed on a Strength or Dexterity saving throw against the Mimic Save DC (the escape DC). On a failed save, the creature takes 1d8 Piercing damage and gains the **Grappled** condition held by the mimic.
* _**Secure Vault:**_ Items stored inside your tame Mimic are immune to liquid, fire, and acid damage. The mimic suppresses its digestive acids unless you command it to dissolve a specific object.

#### Adult (Medium / Very Rare)

* _**Independent Guardian:**_ In combat, your tame Mimic rolls its own **Initiative** and acts on its own turn as an independent ally using the **Mimic** stat block while still following commands.
* _**Campsite Sentry:**_ While in object form, your tame Mimic has Darkvision (60 ft) and Tremorsense (60 ft).

#### Elder (Large / Legendary)

* _**Aberration Transport:**_ Your tame Mimic acts as an independent ally using the **Otyugh** or **Roper** stat block (your choice upon attunement). It can carry up to 4 Medium riders safely inside or on top of its body without reducing its Speed.
* _**All-Terrain Passage:**_ While functioning as a vehicle (such as a wagon or carriage), the elder mimic ignores **Difficult Terrain** caused by mud, dense foliage, or rubble as its underside forms shifting pseudopods to maintain momentum.

#### Hoard (Huge / Artifact)

* _**Territorial Apex:**_ Your tame Mimic acts as an independent ally using the **Hoard Mimic** stat block, guarding a defined area up to a 100-foot cube on your behalf.
* _**Aberration Shelter:**_ Your tame Mimic can form an enclosed, weather-proof structure (such as a cottage or vault).
