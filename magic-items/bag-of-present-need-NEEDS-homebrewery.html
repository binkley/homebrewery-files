Do you need a [items]ladder[/items], some [items]caltrops[/items], an [items]exotic saddle[/items], or a change into [items]fine clothes[/items], and forgot those in your [items]backpack[/items]?

Have no worries! This fine item fills your shopping needs. Pay the bag and any Common item of your exact asking appears fresh and ready to use including fish or unpriced spell components.

The bag only accepts payment or produces items. Attempts to enter or place anything other than payment in the bag fail. Payment is nonrecoverable.

Produce Item. Use a [action]Magic[/action] action to put 99 gp or item(s) of equal or greater value into the bag’s mouth. Clearly speak the item you want the bag to produce. The item appears in a space next to you at the start of your next turn. You may use this feature 2 times [rules]per day[/rules].

Fine Print. If your naming of the item not exact, is not Common, costs 100 gp or more, or does not fit the mouth of the bag, you may get a different item or nothing. The bag is picky about preciseness. You can freely reshape the bag mouth up to a line of 6 feet long or to a circle 3 feet in diameter. All items are at DM discretion including misnamed requests. The bag consumes your payment regardless of outcome. No refunds or warrantees are available.
