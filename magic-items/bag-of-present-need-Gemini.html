Do you need a [items]ladder[/items], some [items]caltrops[/items], an [items]exotic saddle[/items], or a change into [items]fine clothes[/items], but forgot them in your [items]backpack[/items]? Have no worries! This fine item fills your shopping needs.

The bag’s opening can be reshaped as an object interaction, allowing it to stretch into a line up to 6 feet long or a circle up to 3 feet in diameter. The bag accepts only currency or items offered as payment; any attempt to place another object or creature into the bag automatically fails. All payments are nonrecoverable.

Produce Item. As a [actions]Magic[/actions] action, you can place coins or items worth a total of 99 gp or more into the bag’s mouth and clearly speak the name of any mundane item, piece of nonmagical equipment, or spell component that doesn’t have a specific gold piece cost. At the start of your next turn, the requested item appears in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of you.

Once you use this property twice, you can't do so again until you finish a [rules]Long Rest[/rules].

Fine Print. If your naming of the item is inexact, if the item has a value of 100 gp or more, or if it cannot physically fit through the mouth of the bag, the payment is still consumed, but the bag produces nothing or a completely different item determined by the DM. No refunds or warranties are available.
