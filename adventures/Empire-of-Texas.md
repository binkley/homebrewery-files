# The Empire of Texas
Texas: A Post-Fall Fantasy Campaign Setting

Set in a different future, this campaign blends late Medieval and early Renaissance feudalism with remnants of pre-fall technology.

## The Geopolitical Realm
* **The Empire of Texas:** A fragmented, human-dominated empire in name only—comprising rump Texas, rump Oklahoma, and western Louisiana.
* **The Empire of Mexico:** A secretive, closed empire while remains largely unknown.
* **New Mexico:** A high-magic, otherworldly neighbor dominated by High Elves who hold Old West Texas and treat other species with disdain.
* **Eastern Louisiana:** A low-magic, hostile neighbor of impassable swamps and barbaric marsh clans. Expanding the borders of Texas has had little success here.
* **Oklahoma Marches:** Former Indian territories shamanistic and under no regional power. Parts of old Oklahoma are nominally in the Empire of Texas.
* **Borderlands &amp; Independent Powers:** Places like Norlens or El Vado are under city-state control or no control at all. Texas maintains key exclave garrisons in these regions.
* **Distant Powers & Trade Partners:** Kanzas and Missoura to the north; Norlens to the east; Yucata and Havana across the Gulf; strange metals from the Iron Reach

## Key Landscape Features

### Balcones Escarpment (The Shield)
The dramatic elevation jump along the Balcones Fault creates a natural stone rampart through the heart of the state. Sa’atone sits right at this drop-off. In a feudal setting, this escarpment is the **Great Wall of the West**, allowing Texas to easily defend the fertile lowlands against Elven rangers dropping down from the Hill Country.

### Artesian Spring Belt (The Belt)
The fault line unleashes the Edwards Aquifer in massive, pressurized springs—Comal, San Marcos, Barton, and San Pedro. Controlling these high-volume springs means controlling all regional trade, agriculture, and water power for millwheels. A baron holding the San Marcos springhead holds a permanent economic chokehold over surrounding fiefs.

### Llano Uplift & Enchanted Rock
A billion-year-old pre-Cambrian granite batholith north of Sa’atone rich in iron ore, rare earth minerals, and pegmatite dikes. This is the prime **Artificer Crucible** of Texas—a region of massive pink stone fortresses, iron mines, and ancient granite domes where pre-fall tech or high-tier forge magic is housed.

### Coastal Salt Domes (The Deep Vaults)
Subsurface salt domes near the Gulf coast. In a late medieval economy, salt is worth its weight in silver for food preservation. Hollowed-out salt domes make dry, virtually indestructible subterranean vaults for storing ancient pre-fall machinery, black powder reserves, or contraband away from coastal dampness.

### The Caprock Escarpment
A sheer, 1,000-foot wall separating the rolling plains from the High Plains (Panhandle). It serves as a natural border separating human Texan fiefs from the nomadic shamanistic territories in Oklahoma and West Texas.

### Big Bend
The Chisos Mountains and Big Bend basin remain a Texan redoubt surrounded by Elven-claimed West Texas, relying on secret mountain paths and trade routes with Mexican smugglers to survive.

### The Trans-Pecos Mountain Sanctuaries
The Davis and Guadalupe mountains act as high-altitude extensions of the Rocky Mountains held by New Mexico. High Elves use these cooler alpine peaks as high-magic strongholds commanding the arid desert floor.

### Western River Borders
The Pecos River forms a natural moat between the central Texan brushland and the Elven West. The **Rio Grand** separates the Empire of Mexico from Texas and New Mexico.

### The Marches & Piney Woods
To the east, the rugged Ouachita Mountains and Piney Woods form a natural border. Western Louisiana sits on drier timberland easily ruled by Texan knights, while Oklahoma remains an unfortified frontier.

### The Moat (Atchafalaya)
The true eastern barrier is the Atchafalaya Basin. While Spanglish is the common tongue of Texas, Eastern Louisiana decays into an impassable maze of toxic bayous and bottomland hardwood swamps where armored armies drown.

## Settlements of the Realm

### Core Fiefdoms of Texas

#### Ostin (formerly Austin)
Perched on the Balcones Escarpment straddling the Colorado River. The Imperial Capital. The Grand Council and the Emperor sit in the **Pink Citadel**—a massive fortress constructed from Llano granite. Controls the primary Colorado River bridge and Barton Springs.

#### Metro (formerly Dallas & Fort Worth)
Twin rival strongholds along the Trinity River. **Futworth** is a stockade fortress of heavy cavalry and cattle barons; **Dallas** is a wealthy trade city built among skeletal skyscraper frames, ruled by banker-nobles.

#### The Ring-Cities of Houston (The Beltway Barons)
Fortified, bridge-mounted city-states suspended on the elevated flyovers of Loop 610 and Beltway 8 above a flooded bayou. Beneath lies **Grave City**—the toxic, submerged ruins of downtown Houston explored by salvage divers and artificers. Petroleum is refined in the east for pre-fall magic.

#### Iron-Dome (Llano / Enchanted Rock)
A smoke-choked fortress city built into pink granite batholiths. Powered by geothermal vents, its blast furnaces forge black-powder cannons, spring-steel armor, and refined alloys.

#### Shreve Fort (formerly Shreveport)
A heavy timber-and-stone bastion in the Piney Woods. Guards the eastern frontier against swamp incursions across the Atchafalaya.

#### Viktoria (formerly Victoria)
A fortified prairie stronghold along the Guadalupe River. Serves as the Empire’s southern staging ground, cattle hub, and trade junction.

#### Port Corpus (formerly Corpus Christi)
A heavily fortified naval harbor on Corpus Christi Bay protected by Padre Island forts. Builds armed coastal galleys and guards sea lanes against Gulf privateers.

#### Beeville
The southernmost militarized outpost before the lawless Brush Marches. Built on an ancient airfield, it serves as a war-engine depot supplied with pitch and petroleum from Three Rivers.

#### Boca Chica ("The Star-Gantries")
A contested coastal enclave towering over salt marshes. Stainless-steel launch gantries and silos hold pristine aerospace alloys and power cells, guarded by lethal pre-fall automated drones.

### Southern Borderlands

#### El Vado (formerly Laredo)
An independent Free City sitting astride the narrowest low-water crossing of the Rio Grand. Ruled by a council of merchant-smugglers, it serves as a neutral market between Texas and Mexico.

#### Browns Delta
A stilted boomtown near the mouth of the Rio Grand. A lawless haven for scavengers, river pirates, and pilgrims, governed informally by *Las Hermandades del Río*.

### Western Frontiers & Elven Strongholds

#### The Chisos Citadel (Big Bend Redoubt)
An isolated volcanic bowl accessible only through narrow mountain passes like *The Window*. A forward Texan enclave relying on river smugglers to endure.

#### El Pazo (formerly El Paso)
Controlled by the Sun-Elves of New Mexico. Built with white stone and solar-glass spires commanding the Franklin Mountain pass. Humans are strictly barred.

#### The Guadalupe Spires
An arcane watchtower complex carved into the sheer limestone cliffs of El Capitan, garrisoned by Elven rangers and griffon-riders.

### Independent Powers

#### Redwater (The Red River Market)
A sprawling boomtown built on wooden stilts along the Red River. The primary trading post between Texan merchants and nomadic tribal confederacies.

#### Norlens (formerly New Orleans)
An independent, canal-riddled merchant republic ruled by river-guilds and privateers. Defended by sea-walls and mercenary fleets, it has repelled every Texan conquest attempt.

#### Iron Reach (former Cincinatti)

Heavy barges come down the Ohio and M'sippi rivers through Norlens where it is heavily taxed, bringing coal, heavy iron castings, copper, grain, and whiskey in exchange for coastal salt, petroleum fuel, and southern leathers.

#### Rockies (former Colorado and Wyoming)

Long-distance armed wagon trains travel north through Kansas before turning west along the Arkansas River, deliberately skirting Elven and bandit territories. From here come gold, raw silver, gemstones, heavy mountain pelts, and rare pine resin in exchange for lowland grain, salt, and black-powder weapons.

### Ozarks (former Arkansas)

Shunned by most for lawlessness, goods can include lead, zinc, hardwood timber, medicinal herbs, and stone in exchange for Texan salt, refined oil, and woven cotton.

### The Empire of Mexico

Rarely goods come from this secretive empire. Smugglers may pass the ancient Camino Real inland road running south through the neutral free city of El Vado (Laredo) and carry minted silver coin, cochineal dyes, fine leatherwork, and obsidian in exchange for Texan horses, salt, and salvaged pre-times machinery, the later especially prized.

## Faith & Cross-Border Pilgrimages

Cross-border pilgrimages serve as vital diplomatic valves between Texas and neighboring realms, protected by ancient holy traditions.

### Major Pilgrimage Sites

* **The High Shrine of El Capitan (New Mexico):** High Elves permit human pilgrims three weeks during the Autumn Equinox to walk the *Camino Sun-Path* to a cliff-top chapel. Straying more than 10 yards off the path results in immediate execution.
* **Our Lady of Topo Chico (Mexican Border):** A coastal monastery at a rusted pre-fall bottling plant and rocket tower. Pilgrims offer fragments of ancient green glass to pray for safe journeys and rain.
* **The Basilica of Prompt Succor (Norlens):** A sea-walled stone cathedral where river traders make the *Great River Vow* for safe passage through swamp monster waters.
* **Medicine Mounts of Wichita (Oklahoma):** Iron-rich granite peaks operating under the *Truce of the Red Earth*—a weapon-free zone where Texan paladins and shamanic leaders negotiate treaties.
* **Our Lady of Guadalupe (Mexico Interior):** Houses the *Star-Mantle*—a cloak of indestructible pre-fall fiber with glowing constellation threads. Penitents in indigo cloaks may cross the Rio Grand to travel the *Camino Real* unmolested deep into Mexico.

## Starter Hub: Sa’atone

Perched at the base of the Balcones Escarpment, **Sa’atone** (San Antonio) is a bustling oasis city dominating river trade, grain production, and southern defense.

### Key City Districts

1. **The Stonecut Market (The River Cut):** A limestone canyon carved by the river, shaded by collapsed concrete highway flyovers. Features shopfronts cut into stone cliffs and *The Glass Barge*—a neutral tavern barge.
2. **The Alamo Citadel:** A stone fortress clad in scavenged armor plates, serving as the monastery of **The Remnant Guard**.
3. **The Aquifer Vaults:** 150-foot-deep subterranean flood tunnels run by **The Order of the Wellspring**. Water-artificers control the ancient pumps and sluice gates.
4. **The Mission Fiefdoms:** Fortified agricultural manors (San José, Espada) ruled by **Acequia Barons** who control irrigation canals.
5. **The Sun-Crag:** The elevated limestone ridge above the gorge where wealthy merchants and officers reside.

### Prominent Local NPCs
* **Grand Marshal Valeria Vance:** Grim commander of the Remnant Guard at the Alamo.
* **High Hydromancer Hector Cruz:** Reclusive leader of the Order of the Wellspring.
* **Baroness Ysabel de Espada:** Ambitious ruler of Mission Espada.
* **"Gutter-Jack" Reyes:** Affable chief of the River Cut Smugglers' Guild.
